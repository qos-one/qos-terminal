# QOS Trademark Policy

> **DRAFT — for legal review.** Replace the bracketed placeholders before publishing.

QOS is open source, but its name and logo are not. This policy explains how you may use the
**QOS** and **qos.one** names and the **QOS logo** (the "Marks"), owned by **Seksin Cheevirot**
("we"). The code licenses (AGPL-3.0 and Apache-2.0) cover copyright only; they do not grant any
trademark rights.

The goal is simple: anyone should be able to tell whether something comes from the official QOS
project and qos.one, or from someone else.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to QOS truthfully.** "Works with QOS", "built with the QOS SDK", "a fork of QOS",
  "QOS agent for EUR/USD", in articles, talks, tutorials, courses and reviews.
- **Distribute unmodified official releases** under the name QOS, with the logo, as long as you
  do not change the software or suggest you are the official source.
- **Name your agent or strategy package** with a descriptive phrase such as "RSI Reversion for
  QOS" — but not "QOS RSI" or anything that reads like an official product.
- **Use the logo** to link to qos.one or the official repository, unaltered and without implying
  endorsement.

## You may not, without written permission

- **Use the Marks for a modified version** (a fork) or a product built on QOS. Forks must use a
  different name and logo, and must remove ours (for example `assets/icon/` and
  `assets/qos-logo.png` in the Terminal).
- **Offer a hosted service, marketplace, data feed or support offering under the Marks**, or in a
  way that suggests it is run or approved by qos.one.
- **Use the Marks in company, product, domain or social-media names**, such as "QOS Pro",
  "qosmarket.com" or "@QOSsignals".
- **Imply endorsement, partnership or certification** ("official", "certified", "verified by
  QOS") unless we have agreed to it in writing. Only agents listed on qos.one may say they are
  verified by qos.one.
- **Alter the logo** (colours, proportions, combining it with other marks) or use confusingly
  similar names or logos.
- **Use the Marks in connection with financial promotions**, return promises or investment
  advice.

## Forks

Forking is welcome — that is what open source is for. Please:

1. Choose a new name and logo and remove ours.
2. Keep the copyright notices and license files.
3. Point the Cloud URL to your own service or leave it unset; do not present your service as
   qos.one.
4. You may say "based on QOS" in your description.

## Reporting and permission requests

Write to **seksin.cheevirot@gmail.com**. We may update this policy; the current version is at
**https://qos.one/legal/trademarks**.

This policy is based on common open-source trademark practice. It is not legal advice.
